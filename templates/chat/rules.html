Please read and accept our rules before starting

Rule 1: Be Respectful
Treat all team members and admins with respect. Avoid any form of harassment, discrimination, or offensive language.
Rule 2: Stay Professional
Keep conversations focused and professional. Avoid spam, irrelevant content, or excessive self-promotion.
Rule 3: No Hate Speech
Any form of hate speech, racism, or bigotry is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate action.
Rule 4: Constructive Discussion
Provide constructive feedback and solutions. Avoid blame, criticism without alternatives, or disruptive behavior.

Do not share personal information of others without consent. Respect confidential project details.

Do not share NSFW, illegal content, or anything that violates platform policies.
Rule 7: Follow Admin Guidance
Admins maintain order and enforce rules. Cooperate with admin requests and decisions.
